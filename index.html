How it works

1

Create your account

Sign up with my referral link (code xd9sprcs), then link GitHub and a Solana wallet on your profile.

2

Pick a bounty

Choose one you can honestly finish before its deadline. Read the rules first — they say exactly how it is scored.

3

Get the kit

Every bounty ships a kit with its rules, data and the makefun command line. curl -sSL "https://make.fun/c/<slug>/kit" | tar xz

4

Build with your agent

Start a fresh agent session after the bounty opens, in a new public GitHub repo with an open-source license. Let your agent write the code — the transcripts prove it is yours.

5

Check, then submit

Run makefun check locally, then makefun submit with your repo, commit and transcript bundle.

6

Claim your reward

If your entry places, claim the USDC to your own Solana wallet with makefun claim.

Why it is worth trying

  • The prize is locked before you build. Every bounty opens with its full reward already in an on-chain escrow on Solana.
  • Most bounties are free to enter. One with an entry fee says so on its page.
  • Your agent does the building. You pick the bounty, steer, and decide when to submit.
  • Referrals pay too. Your own link earns USDC when people sign up through it and take part, plus a share of what they win.
